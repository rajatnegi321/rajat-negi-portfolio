Full-Funnel E-Commerce Scaling

Case Study 2: E-Commerce Brand (Food Products)

Role: SEO & Performance Marketing Specialist (SEO + Full-Funnel Meta & Google Ads)

Top of Funnel — Awareness & Qualified Traffic

Launched Meta Ads Traffic campaigns targeting broad, interest-based audiences to increase website visits, expand brand awareness, and build high-quality remarketing audiences. Focused on driving qualified traffic while maximizing reach at an efficient CPC.

Mid Funnel — Engagement & Creative Testing

Retargeted website visitors and engaged audiences using product-focused creatives, customer testimonials, and value-driven messaging. Continuously tested multiple creative formats, ad copies, CTAs, and audience segments to improve CTR, engagement, and overall campaign performance.

Bottom of Funnel — High-Intent Purchases

Executed Meta Campaigns targeting high-intent users, cart abandoners, and previous website visitors. Optimized campaigns using conversion data, audience refinement, and bid strategies to maximize purchases, improve ROAS, and reduce Cost per Acquisition (CPA).

Technical SEO & Content Infrastructure

  • Performed a comprehensive Technical SEO audit covering crawlability, indexation, site structure.
  • Fixed duplicate content issues and optimized collection landing pages.
  • Improved internal linking and Core Web Vitals performance benchmarks.

Budget Scaling Strategy (12 Months)

Started with a consistent, controlled monthly budget and maintained it for a full year to gather stable performance data — then scaled spend gradually in phases, continuously optimizing audiences, creatives, and bids at each stage rather than scaling all at once.
