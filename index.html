Paid Media — Full-Funnel Strategy

Top of Funnel — Awareness & Traffic:

Launched Meta Ads Traffic campaigns targeting broad, interest-based audiences to increase website visits, expand brand awareness, and build high-quality remarketing audiences. Focused on driving qualified traffic while maximizing reach at an efficient CPC.

Mid Funnel — Engagement & Creative Testing:

Retargeted website visitors and engaged audiences using product-focused creatives, testimonials, and value-driven messaging. Continuously tested multiple creative formats, ad copies, CTAs, and audience segments to improve CTR, engagement, and overall campaign performance.

Bottom of Funnel — Purchases:

Executed Meta Campaigns targeting high - intent users , cart abandoners and previous website visitores. Optimized campaigns using conversion data, audience refinement, and bid strategies to maximize purchases, improve ROAS, and reduce Cost per Acquisition (CPA)

Budget Scaling:

Started with a consistent, controlled monthly budget and maintained it for a full year to gather stable performance data — then scaled spend gradually in phases, continuously optimizing audiences, creatives, and bids at each stage rather than scaling all at once.
